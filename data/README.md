# Reference data

## `bbr-roads-reference.geojson`

- **What:** 1,269 road lines around Bandar Bukit Raja, imported from OpenStreetMap during the hackathon.
- **Use:** tests, screenshots and design mockups only.
- **Not read by the app:** the app reads `public/data/bbr-roads.geojson`, which stays on the owner's PC.
- **Not verified:** these lines are not checked road conditions, closures or travel advice.

**Licence:** map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL). Keep this attribution wherever the data is shown.

**Origin:** this file used to live at `judge-demo/assets/bbr-roads.geojson`. It moved here when the public judge demo was retired on 10 November 2026. Its contents are unchanged.
